# Learning path and common beginner mistakes

Use this when the user asks "how do I learn investing", "what should I read" or "what
mistakes do beginners make". Present it in plain Polish, a few points at a time, not as a
wall of text.

## Ten short lessons (in this order)

| # | Topic | Skill to practise with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Budget, surplus and the emergency fund | `gpw-start` |
| 2 | What shares, bonds, funds and ETFs are; what an index is | glossary |
| 3 | Risk and return: why higher expected return always comes with deeper falls | `gpw-start` (equity range) |
| 4 | Costs: commissions, minimum fees, TER; why 1% a year matters over 25 years | `gpw-account-planner` (cost parameter) |
| 5 | Taxes and accounts: Belka tax, PIT-38, IKE, IKZE, OKI | `gpw-account-planner` |
| 6 | Diversification and a simple portfolio (e.g. world equities + Polish equities + bonds) | `gpw-etf-planner list` |
| 7 | Regular contributions (DCA) and rebalancing | `gpw-etf-planner plan` |
| 8 | Reading a portfolio: concentration, correlation, drawdown | `gpw-portfolio-check` |
| 9 | (Optional) Single stocks: thesis, stop, position size, journal, paper mode first | `gpw-trade-journal --paper`, `gpw-position-sizer` |
| 10 | (Optional) Market context: breadth, sectors, regime, and why timing is hard | `gpw-market-breadth`, `gpw-market-regime` |

## The most common beginner mistakes

1. **Investing money needed soon.** Money for a flat deposit in 2 years does not belong in shares.
2. **No emergency fund.** The first unexpected bill forces a sale at a loss.
3. **Concentration.** Everything goes into one or two "sure" companies, often the employer or a forum favourite.
4. **Chasing performance.** Buying whatever rose most last year: last year's winner is not next year's.
5. **Market timing.** Waiting for "the bottom" or selling "before the crash". Missing a few of the best sessions cuts long-run returns sharply.
6. **Leveraged and short ETFs held for months.** Daily-reset products lose value in choppy markets even when the index ends flat.
7. **Ignoring costs.** Small purchases with a 5 zł minimum fee; funds with a 2–3% annual fee instead of 0.1–0.6% ETFs.
8. **Overtrading.** Every trade costs commission and, on a regular account, triggers tax.
9. **Panic selling after a fall.** This turns a temporary loss into a permanent one. The plan should be written before the fall.
10. **Tips, influencers and "guaranteed" offers.** Check every firm in the KNF register and on its warning list.
